WHO WE ARE

About TRÓPICO

TRÓPICO is a research group dedicated to understanding tropical atmospheric, climatic, and hydrological processes. Our work explores the physical mechanisms governing tropical weather and climate, their variability across different spatial and temporal scales, and their implications for extreme weather and hydrometeorological hazards.

WHAT WE STUDY

Research Areas

Tropical Atmospheric Dynamics

Atmospheric circulation, organized convection, and tropical waves.

Tropical Climatology

Climate variability, climate change, and tropical climate dynamics.

Hydrology and Hydroclimatology

Precipitation, atmospheric moisture transport, and hydrological variability.

Tropical Meteorology

Tropical cyclones, mesoscale convective systems, and extreme weather.

Hydrometeorological Hazards

Extreme precipitation, droughts, heat waves, and lightning.
